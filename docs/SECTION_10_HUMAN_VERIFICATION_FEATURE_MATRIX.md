| 16 | **Escalate Audit** | Route complex or contested packaging cases to Senior Officers or Legal Counsel | `POST /api/reviews/{id}/escalate`<br>`escalate_review()` | **COMPLETE** | `test_escalate_and_reopen_service` |
| 17 | **Assign Audit to Officer** | Officer workload delegation, dynamic assignment, re-assignment, and ownership tracking | `POST /api/reviews/{id}/assign`<br>`assign_review()` | **COMPLETE** | `test_api_review_lifecycle_endpoints` |
| 18 | **Officer Workload Dashboard** | Aggregated workload distribution per officer (Pending, In Review, Completed, Escalated) | `GET /api/reviews/officers`<br>`get_officer_dashboard_summary()` | **COMPLETE** | `test_api_officer_workload_and_directory` |
